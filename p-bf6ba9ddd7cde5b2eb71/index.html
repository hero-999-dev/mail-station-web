Test_Mail_Station

All your mailboxes in one window, filed by platform: Gmail, Yandex, Proton, Outlook & Hotmail and others.

What it is

Test_Mail_Station is a personal mail reader for Windows and Android. It connects to your mailboxes over IMAP and shows them together or one platform at a time. It reads mail; it does not send mail.

How it uses your Google account

  • When you choose "Sign in with Google", Google's own sign-in page opens and asks you to allow Test_Mail_Station to read your Gmail over IMAP (scope https://mail.google.com/).
  • The app runs entirely on your device. Your mail goes directly between your device and Google; there is no Test_Mail_Station server and no cloud service.
  • Nothing is collected, shared, sold or used for advertising.
