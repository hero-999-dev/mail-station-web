Privacy policy

Test_Mail_Station - effective 15 November 2026

Summary

Test_Mail_Station runs only on your own device. It has no server, no user accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. The developer never receives your mail, your address or your credentials.

Data the app accesses

  • Mail: with your permission the app reads messages, folders and attachments of the mailboxes you connect, over IMAP, so it can show them to you. For Gmail it uses the Google scope https://mail.google.com/.
  • Mailbox list and settings: the addresses of the mailboxes you added and your settings, kept on your device.

How the data is stored

  • Mail is fetched only while you use the app and is not written to disk. An attachment is saved only when you click it, to a place on your device.
  • Passwords are never stored. A Google sign-in returns a refresh token; on Windows it is kept encrypted on your device until you sign out, elsewhere only in memory while the app runs.

Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone. Mail travels only between your device and your mail provider (for Gmail: Google).

Google user data

Test_Mail_Station's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to show your own mail to you inside the app; it is not used for advertising, not read by people and not used to train AI models.

Removing access

Press SIGN OUT in the app to delete the stored token. You can also remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: use the user support e-mail shown on Google's sign-in screen for Test_Mail_Station.
